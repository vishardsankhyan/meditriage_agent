=========================================================
MEDITRIAGE CLINICAL PROTOCOL & SPECIALTY ROUTING DIRECTIVE
Document ID: MTP-2026-REV4
Effective Date: September 2026
=========================================================

1. ORTHOPEDIC SPECIALTY ROUTING:
- If a patient reports joint clicking, locking, or mechanical instability (e.g., knee or shoulder clicking) combined with a self-reported pain level of 7 or higher, the agent must categorize the case under "Orthopedic Specialist Queue" and flag for expedited outpatient imaging (MRI).

2. CARDIAC EMERGENCY PROTOCOL (RED ALERT):
- Any mention of chest tightness, pressure, substernal burning, or pain radiating to the left arm or jaw must immediately override standard routing. 
- Regardless of the user's numeric pain rating, the agent must instantly trigger the `escalate_to_human` tool and transfer the call to the On-Call Cardiology ER team.

3. NEUROLOGICAL SCREENING:
- If a patient complains of a sudden severe headache ("thunderclap headache") accompanied by dizziness, vertigo, or visual disturbances, the agent must classify the case as "Neurological Priority" and recommend immediate emergency department evaluation.

4. PEDIATRIC & GENERAL FEVER GUIDELINES:
- For patients reporting high fever (>103°F or 39.4°C) combined with lethargy or confusion, the agent must flag the record as urgent and advise immediate virtual or in-person physician consultation within 2 hours.

5. CHRONIC PAIN MANAGEMENT:
- For returning patients with chronic lower back or limb pain whose pain level has remained stable (±1 point from previous visit), route to Routine Outpatient Physical Therapy follow-up rather than emergency escalation.
